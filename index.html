About Vijay

Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar, widely known as Vijay and popularly called "Thalapathy", built a long career in Tamil cinema before entering full-time politics.

From child artist to leading star

Vijay appeared as a child artist in films directed by his father, filmmaker S. A. Chandrasekhar, and later became a leading actor. Over the following decades he developed a major mass following through commercial, action, drama and family films.

He has also worked as a playback singer and is associated with popular titles such as Thuppakki, Kaththi, Mersal, Master, Leo, The Greatest of All Time and Jana Nayagan.

The Move Into Public Life

Party

Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam

Vijay announced the formation of Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam in February 2024, marking his formal entry into electoral politics. He said he intended to focus fully on politics after completing his remaining film commitments.

2026

Assembly election & government

In the 2026 Tamil Nadu Assembly election, TVK emerged as the largest party. Vijay subsequently became Chief Minister with support from allied parties.

Current Office

Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu

C. Joseph Vijay was sworn in as Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu on 10 May 2026 at the Nehru Indoor Stadium in Chennai. The oath was administered by Governor Rajendra Vishwanath Arlekar.
